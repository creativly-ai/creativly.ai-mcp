# Briefs to try

Start with a connected account and an approved project. These are normal creative prompts; the agent handles model/tool parameters and checks the quote and connection limits.

## Product launch

> Use Creativly to create a launch image for a moss-green trail shoe on rain-dark volcanic rock. Give it a strong editorial feel, with a bright orange lace and low mist behind it.

Follow up:

> Make the lighting a little moodier. Keep the shoe and the orange lace.

## A paper character

> Create a little red folded-paper crab on a pale blue tabletop. Make the folds and cut-paper texture visible, with soft studio shadows and a playful handmade feel.

Follow up:

> Animate it for six seconds. Have it shuffle in place and turn toward the camera with a handmade stop-motion rhythm.

## A still that moves

Choose a bakery image in your project, or upload one through the picker:

> Turn this bakery photo into a six-second film. Gently pull the croissant apart so a few crisp flakes fall through the warm morning light.

## Change the mood of a clip

Choose an owned product video:

> Keep the product and camera movement, but make the lighting feel like a warm late-evening studio shoot.

## Two images, then their films

> Make two product-launch images for a fictional citrus soda: one crisp studio shot and one outdoors at sunset. Keep them together in this session.

Then:

> Animate both as short product films. Keep the original images alongside the videos so I can compare them.

## Use an edited first frame

Upload your source clip and a prepared frame:

> Restyle this clip to match the edited first frame. Keep the original action and camera timing.

The chosen video-edit model must support both inputs. Ask for the current compatible model and settings if unsure.

## Come back tomorrow

> Find the session where we made the trail-shoe launch. Show the original image, any edited versions and the videos.
