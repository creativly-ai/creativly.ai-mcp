# A few things to make in a conversation

An editorial product launch, a handmade paper character and a warm bakery film: three briefs to explore with a connected agent.

These are public Creativly showcase assets. Images were made with Nano Banana 2.1 and the six-second films with Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash. Click a film poster to open the original MP4.

## Trail launch

![A moss-green trail shoe with orange laces on wet volcanic rock, with low mist and pine trees behind.](https://cdn.creativly.ai/mcp/examples/2026-11-originals/trail-launch-nano-banana-2-1-native-1a00a119ff1d01b4.webp)

**Brief**

> Create a launch image for a moss-green trail shoe on rain-dark volcanic rock. Give it a strong editorial feel, with a bright orange lace and low mist behind it.
